# No figure without its period and source

Every published figure carries its fiscal year, quarter, published date and source filing, and a figure whose provenance is missing is shown as "not recorded" rather than hidden or guessed. This came from running an investment plan for four months on a wrong bank NPL and twice mistaking an annualized monsoon-quarter EPS for a full year. The exchange exposes the submitted date and filing path for every report, so the cost is two columns and a backfill.
